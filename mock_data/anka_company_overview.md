# Anka Endüstriyel Otomasyon A.Ş. - Company Overview & Knowledge Bundle

## Executive Summary & Company Profile
Anka Endüstriyel Otomasyon A.Ş. ("Anka Industrial Automation Inc.") is a premier Turkish technology and engineering company specializing in industrial automation systems, Industry 4.0 software platforms, high-performance PLCs, Variable Frequency Drives (VFDs), human-machine interfaces (HMIs), IoT sensors, and turnkey robotic cells. Headquartered in Kocaeli with a major engineering hub in Bursa, Anka serves over 1,200 industrial customers across Turkey and Southeastern Europe.

## Industrial Automation Solutions & Product Offerings
Anka provides end-to-end industrial automation and digital transformation solutions tailored for heavy industrial and manufacturing environments:

1. **Anka-PLC-5000 Series**: Next-generation modular Programmable Logic Controllers engineered for high-speed, multi-axis industrial motion control and factory floor automation.
2. **AnkaDrive-X VFDs**: Heavy-duty Variable Frequency Drives delivering high torque control and energy savings up to 35% for motor-driven industrial equipment and pumps.
3. **AnkaTouch-10 Industrial HMI**: 10.1-inch IP65 widescreen touch panels built for harsh factory floor environments, providing multi-protocol PLC connectivity and real-time visualization.
4. **AnkaSCADA v4.2 Platform**: Cloud-native Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition software featuring real-time telemetry analytics, OPC-UA protocol support, alarm logging, and ERP integration.
5. **AnkaSense IoT Sensor Node Network**: Wireless 3-axis vibration and temperature sensor nodes operating over industrial LPWAN/LoRaWAN for predictive maintenance of rotating machinery.
6. **Robotic Cell Solutions**: Modular 6-axis industrial robot automation cells customized for precision welding, automated assembly, palletizing, and material handling.
7. **SLA Contracts & Engineering Field Support**: Tiered maintenance contracts (Gold 24/7 1-hour response, Silver 12-hour response, Bronze 48-hour response) backed by expert field automation engineers.

## Key Turkish Manufacturing Hubs & Customer Base
Anka's field operations, regional service centers, and sales networks are strategically positioned across major industrial hubs in Turkey:

- **Bursa Automotive & Machinery Hub**: Serving major automotive OEMs (Tofaş, Renault suppliers) and textile machinery manufacturers in Bursa Organize Sanayi Bölgesi (BOSB) and Demirtaş OSB.
- **Kocaeli Industrial & Chemical Corridor**: Serving automotive giants (Ford Otosan), tire manufacturers (Kordsa), chemical plants, and heavy machinery fabricators in Gebze OSB, TOSB Automotive Supply Industry Zone, and Dilovası OSB.
- **Manisa & İzmir Aegean Electronics Hub**: Partnering with white goods and appliance leaders (Vestel, Indesit suppliers) in Manisa Industrial Zone and İzmir Atatürk OSB.
- **Istanbul Desbaş & Tuzla Industrial Belt**: Providing automation software, telemetry, and marine automation solutions in Tuzla Shipyard Zone and Desbaş Free Zone.
- **Konya Machinery Cluster**: Delivering PLC motion control kits and drive packages to agricultural machinery and machine tool manufacturers in Konya Industrial Zone.
- **Ankara Ostim & Sincan OSB**: Supporting defense industry manufacturing, metal fabrication, and power distribution systems in Ostim and Sincan.
