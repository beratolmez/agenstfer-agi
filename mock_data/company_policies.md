# Company Policies - TechFlow Inc.

## Support Policy
- Enterprise customers receive 24/7 support with a 1-hour SLA.
- Pro customers receive business-hours support with a 12-hour SLA.
- Basic customers receive email-only support with a 48-hour SLA.

## Refund Policy
- No refunds after 14 days of the billing cycle.
- Annual plans can be canceled anytime, but no pro-rated refunds are provided.

## Sales Discount Policy
- Sales reps can offer up to 10% discount on Enterprise annual plans without manager approval.
- Any discount above 10% requires VP of Sales approval.
