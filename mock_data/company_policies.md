# Company Policies - Anka Endüstriyel Otomasyon A.Ş.

## Support Policy
- **Enterprise / Gold Tier**: Customers receive 24/7 priority support with a 1-hour SLA response time, dedicated technical account manager, and 4-hour on-site technician dispatch for critical line stops.
- **Pro / Silver Tier**: Customers receive business-hours support (08:00–18:00 TRT) with a 12-hour SLA response time and remote diagnostic assistance.
- **Basic / Bronze Tier**: Customers receive email-only support with a 48-hour SLA response time during standard business days.

## Refund Policy
- No refunds for monthly plans after 14 days of the billing cycle.
- **Annual Plans Refund Policy**: Annual software subscriptions and SLA contracts can be canceled at any time, with a **pro-rated policy** applied. Customers receive a pro-rated refund for the remaining full unused months minus a 10% administrative processing fee.

## Sales Discount Policy
- Sales representatives can offer up to a 10% discount on Enterprise annual contracts and hardware bundles (e.g., Anka-PLC-5000, AnkaDrive-X) without prior manager approval.
- Any discount between 10% and 20% requires written approval from the Sales Director / VP of Sales.
- Custom contract terms or discounts above 20% require formal approval from the Chief Financial Officer (CFO).

## SLA Rules & Service Level Agreements
- **Cloud SCADA & Telemetry Availability**: 99.9% guaranteed monthly uptime for AnkaSCADA cloud endpoints.
- **Hardware Warranty & Replacement**: 2-year advance hardware replacement warranty for Anka-PLC-5000, AnkaDrive-X, AnkaTouch-10, and AnkaSense IoT Sensor Nodes under active SLA contracts.
- **Critical Failure SLA**: In the event of total system failure or production line halt, Enterprise SLA guarantees emergency field engineer response within 4 hours in major Turkish industrial hubs (Bursa, Kocaeli, İstanbul, İzmir, Ankara, Konya).
